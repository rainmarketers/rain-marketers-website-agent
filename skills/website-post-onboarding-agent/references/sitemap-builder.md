# Sitemap Builder: Recommended Website Structures by Industry

The Website Brief includes a recommended sitemap (page structure). Use these templates as starting points based on the client's industry.

---

## B2C Services (Home Services, Salons, Fitness, etc.)

**Best for**: Gutter cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, hair salons, fitness studios

```
Homepage
├── About Us
├── Services
│   ├── Service 1 (with pricing)
│   ├── Service 2 (with pricing)
│   └── Service 3 (with pricing)
├── Our Process
├── Testimonials / Case Studies
├── Service Area / Coverage Map
├── FAQ
├── Contact / Book Now
└── Blog (optional)
```

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Problem, solution, differentiator, CTA to book
- **Services**: Each service with pricing, what's included, guarantee
- **Testimonials**: Client reviews, before/afters, ratings
- **Service Area**: Map or list of served areas (local SEO + trust)
- **Contact/Book**: Easy scheduling, multiple contact methods
- **FAQ**: Common objections addressed
- **Blog**: Tips, industry info (builds authority + SEO)

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Homepage: "I need this service"
2. Services page: "Do you handle my specific need?"
3. Testimonials: "Are you trustworthy?"
4. Book/Contact: "Let's schedule"

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Problem statement, solution, results proof
- **Services**: What each service includes, typical results, timeline
- **Case Studies**: Real client examples, metrics, transformation
- **Team/Expertise**: Credentials, experience, specializations
- **Resources/Blog**: Thought leadership, industry insights
- **Contact**: Demo scheduling, discovery call CTA

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Homepage: "I have this business problem"
2. Case Studies: "Have you solved this before?"
3. Team: "Are your people credible?"
4. Contact: "Let's start a conversation"

## B2B SaaS

**Best for**: Software as a Service, tools, platforms

```
Homepage
├── Product Demo / Tour
├── Pricing
├── Features
│   ├── Feature 1
│   ├── Feature 2
│   └── Feature 3
├── Integrations
├── Use Cases / Industries
├── Case Studies / Customers
├── Security / Compliance
├── Documentation / Help
├── Blog / Resources
├── Comparison (vs. competitors, optional)
├── Signup / Free Trial CTA
└── Contact Sales
```

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Problem, solution, key benefits, free trial CTA
- **Pricing**: Clear tiers, what's included, FAQ about pricing
- **Features**: Detailed feature list with screenshots/videos
- **Use Cases**: Different ways to use the product by industry/role
- **Case Studies**: Customer success stories, measurable results
- **Security**: Data protection, compliance, certifications
- **Documentation**: Help center, onboarding resources

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Homepage: "This problem is mine"
2. Pricing/Features: "Is this the right solution?"
3. Case Studies: "Will this work for my company?"
4. Free Trial/Signup: "Let's try it"

```
Homepage

├── Featured / New Products
├── Best Sellers
├── About Us / Brand Story
├── Why Choose Us (unique selling points)
├── Customer Reviews / Testimonials

├── Shipping & Returns
├── FAQ

└── Contact / Support

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Brand story, top products, social proof
- **Category pages**: Filter, sort, compare products
- **Product pages**: Images, description, reviews, size/specs
- **Reviews**: Customer feedback, ratings, photos
- **Shipping & Returns**: Clear policies reduce cart abandonment
- **Contact**: Support channels, live chat

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Homepage: "I like this brand"
2. Category: "Do you have what I want?"
3. Product page: "Is this the right product?"
4. Reviews: "Is this quality/trustworthy?"
5. Checkout: "Buy now"

## Local Services (Retail, Restaurants, Professional Services)

**Best for**: Brick-and-mortar shops, restaurants, professional practices (doctors, dentists, lawyers)

```
Homepage
├── Location / Hours
├── About Us
├── Services / Menu / Specialties
├── Why Choose Us
├── Staff / Team (if applicable)
├── Testimonials / Reviews
├── Photo Gallery
├── Appointment Booking
├── Contact / Directions
└── Local FAQs
```

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Location, hours, what to expect, CTA to book/visit
- **About**: Location story, staff, specialties
- **Services/Menu**: What you offer, pricing
- **Testimonials**: Local reviews, happy customers
- **Appointments**: Online booking, phone, walk-in info
- **Directions**: Map, address, parking info, hours

**Special focus**:
- **Google Business Profile**: This IS your most important "page"
- **Local reviews**: Encourage Google/Yelp reviews
- **Mobile optimization**: Most visits come from mobile searches
- **Location schema**: Help Google understand where you are

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Google search: "I need this, where is it?"
2. Google Business Profile / Homepage: "Is this place legit? Can I get in today?"
3. Reviews: "What do other people think?"
4. Contact/Book: "Let's go there / Book appointment"

**Best for**: News sites, blogs, content publishers, educational sites

```
Homepage
├── Featured / Latest Content
├── Categories / Topics
├── Popular Articles
├── Author / About
├── Newsletter Signup
├── Resources / Tools

```

**Key pages**:
- **Homepage**: Latest articles, trending content, categories
- **Article pages**: Full content, byline, date, related articles
- **Categories**: All articles in a topic, organized by date
- **Author**: Bio, other articles by author
- **Search**: Help readers find content
- **Newsletter**: Email signup for recurring traffic

**Monetization considerations**:
- Sponsorship placement
- Ads or affiliate links
- Membership / paywall
- Premium content tiers

**Conversion funnel**:
1. Homepage / Google: "Is there interesting content here?"
2. Article: "Is this valuable?"
3. Related articles: "What else do you have?"
4. Newsletter signup: "Keep me updated"

## Template Customization

When recommending sitemap in the brief:

1. **Choose the right template** for the industry
2. **Customize sections** based on the client's business
   - Add pages if needed (Resources, Certifications, News, etc.)
   - Remove pages that don't apply
   - Rename pages to match client's language
3. **Prioritize conversion funnel**: Ensure flow makes sense for the customer journey
4. **Add industry-specific pages**: 
   - B2B SaaS might add "Integrations"
   - Local services might add "Directions"
   - E-commerce might add "Size Guide"
5. **Include secondary goals**: Blog for SEO, newsletter for retention, etc.

Homepage

│   ├── Gutter Cleaning

```

- Multiple CTAs (contact, book, call) for different customer preferences

## Sitemap Best Practices

✅ **Do**:
- Keep main navigation to 5-7 items (clean, uncluttered)
- Use clear, simple language for page names
- Ensure logical hierarchy (parent > child pages)
- Include a CTA on every page
- Use breadcrumbs for nested pages
- Include contact/booking on every page (footer if not header)
- Prioritize conversion funnel flow

❌ **Don't**:
- Over-engineer the sitemap (unnecessary pages = dead weight)
- Bury important information 3+ levels deep
- Confuse visitors with unclear navigation
- Hide contact info or CTAs
- Add "Blog" if you won't regularly publish content
- Forget mobile navigation (mobile is ~60% of traffic)

---

## Mobile Sitemap Considerations

Mobile navigation should:
- Be simplified (hamburger menu is fine)
- Show key pages: Home, Services, Contact, Book
- Include click-to-call in header
- Be thumb-friendly (large tap targets)
- Load fast (mobile users have limited bandwidth)
