# Client Onboarding Form Fields

Use this form when onboarding new clients to Rain Marketers. Responses help the Website Agent conduct more targeted market research and positioning strategy.

---

## Onboarding Form

### Section 1: Company Basics

**1. Company Name** (text field)
*What should we call you?*
- Used for Asana project lookup and branding throughout

**2. Website URL** (text field)
*What's your current website?*
- Agent will analyze homepage and content

**3. Business Type** (dropdown)
*What best describes your business?*
- B2B SaaS
- B2B Services (consulting, agencies, etc.)
- B2C Services (home services, salons, fitness, etc.)
- E-commerce (physical products)
- Local Services (retail, restaurants, professional services)
- Hybrid (multiple revenue streams)
- Other: ______

**4. Industry / Vertical** (text field)
*What industry are you in? (e.g., gutter cleaning, marketing agency, software)*

**5. Years in Business** (number field)
*How long have you been operating?*
- Helps inform trust/credibility positioning

---

### Section 2: Target Customer

**6. Primary Target Customer** (text field)
*Who is your ideal customer?*
- Example: "Homeowners aged 35-65 with homes built before 1990"
- Be as specific as possible

**7. Customer Pain Points** (text area)
*What are the top 3-5 pain points your customers have?*
- Example: "Forget to clean gutters, gutters clog causing water damage, don't trust unfamiliar services, can't find reliable help"
- These will inform messaging strategy

**8. Geographic Service Area** (text field)
*Where do you serve? (e.g., "Chicago metro," "National," "Northeast US")*

**9. Typical Customer Decision Criteria** (text area)
*What factors influence your customers' buying decisions?*
- Example: "Price, reputation/reviews, speed of service, warranty/guarantee, local presence"

---

### Section 3: Market & Competition

**10. What Makes You Different?** (text area)
*What do you think is your biggest competitive advantage?*
- Example: "Same-day service, lifetime guarantee, proprietary technology"
- Agent will validate/refine based on market research

**11. Who Do You Consider Your Main Competitors?** (text area)
*Name 3-5 direct competitors (if you know them)*
- If left blank, agent will research to find them

**12. Market Opportunity** (text area)
*How big is the market you're in? Is it growing, shrinking, changing?*
- Leave blank if unsure; agent will research

---

### Section 4: Current Marketing & Goals

**13. Current Marketing Channels** (checkboxes)
*Which of these do you currently use?*
- [ ] Google Ads / SEM
- [ ] Social media (paid)
- [ ] Content marketing / blog
- [ ] Email marketing
- [ ] Referrals / word-of-mouth
- [ ] Local listings (Google Business, Yelp, etc.)
- [ ] Other: ______

**14. Primary Website Goals** (checkboxes)
*What should your website accomplish?*
- [ ] Generate leads (contact form submissions, calls)
- [ ] Sell products/services directly
- [ ] Build brand authority / credibility
- [ ] Educate customers
- [ ] Other: ______

**15. Success Metrics** (text area)
*How will you measure if the new website is working?*
- Example: "100 qualified leads per month, 5% conversion rate from visitor to lead"

**16. Budget & Timeline** (text area)
*What's your budget and timeline for the website project?*
- Example: "Budget: $15K-25K, Timeline: Launch in Q3"

---

### Section 5: Additional Context

**17. Key Business Story** (text area)
*Is there a founder story, company origin, or unique history we should know?*
- Example: "Started as a side business, grew to 12 employees in 3 years through word-of-mouth"
- Helps with trust/credibility positioning

**18. Unique Processes or Technology** (text area)
*Do you have any proprietary processes, technology, or methods?*
- Example: "Developed proprietary gutter inspection camera that reduces time by 50%"

**19. Current Messaging / Tagline** (text field)
*What do you currently say about your business?*
- Example: "The gutter cleaning company that guarantees zero clogs"
- Agent will evaluate and recommend refinements

**20. Anything Else We Should Know?** (text area)
*Any other context about your business, market, or goals?*
- Open-ended catch-all for important context

---

## Instructions for Using Form Responses

### For the Agent:
When reviewing form responses, prioritize:
1. **Specific pain points** (Question 7) – Use to guide research
2. **Claimed differentiation** (Question 10) – Validate against competitors
3. **Market context** (Questions 3, 4, 8) – Scope market research
4. **Goals & metrics** (Questions 14, 15) – Inform KPIs in brief

### For Stevie (Reviewing Output):
When reviewing agent output, check:
- Does the positioning address the pain points mentioned in Q7? ✓
- Does the positioning account for claimed differentiation from Q10? ✓
- Do the KPIs align with success metrics from Q15? ✓
- Is the target customer consistent with Q6? ✓

---

## Form Distribution

**When to send**:
- During initial consultation / sales call
- Before scheduling Website Agent

**Where to send**:
- Email (PDF form) with instructions
- Online form (Typeform, Google Form, Asana form)

**Due date**:
- Within 3 days of contract signing
- If client doesn't respond, agent can still run with website analysis + Google research

**What if customer doesn't fill it out?**
The agent will:
1. Analyze website to infer target customer and positioning
2. Conduct comprehensive market research
3. Note assumptions in the report ("Assuming target is X based on website messaging")
4. Ask for clarification if needed

**Company Name**: Clean Cut Gutters

**Industry**: Gutter Cleaning

- Local presence / recommendations

- Same-day service for most calls

## Form Validation Checklist

Before agent runs, verify form has:
- [ ] Company name (required for Asana lookup)
- [ ] Website URL (required for agent research)
- [ ] At least 3 pain points (helpful for targeting)
- [ ] Claimed differentiators (helps validate positioning)
- [ ] Success metrics or goals (informs KPIs)
- [ ] Contact info to clarify if needed

If critical fields missing:
- Continue with website + market research
- Note assumptions in report
- Ask for clarification if needed during Asana task creation
