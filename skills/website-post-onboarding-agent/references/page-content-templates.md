# Page Content Templates

Ready-to-customize copy frameworks for common website pages. These templates follow the positioning strategy and messaging pillars from the Website Brief.

---

## Homepage Template

```
HERO SECTION
Headline: [Benefit-focused, speaks to primary pain point]
Example: "Never Clean Gutters Again"
Example: "Ship Orders 3x Faster"

Subheadline: [Expand on the benefit, add proof or proof element]
Example: "Our patented gutter system means you'll never climb a ladder again. Same-day cleaning, zero clogs guaranteed."
Example: "Automate your fulfillment. 500+ e-commerce brands trust [Company]."

Hero Image/Video: [Visual showing the benefit in action]

Primary CTA: [Action-oriented, clear next step]
Example: "Schedule Your Free Inspection"
Example: "Start Your Free Trial"

Secondary CTA: [Lower-commitment option]
Example: "See Our Process"
Example: "Watch Demo"

---

VALUE PROPOSITIONS (3-4 blocks)
Each block should follow this structure:

Block 1:
Icon: [Visual representing the benefit]
Headline: [Benefit, not feature]
Example: "Save Time" (benefit) vs. "Fast Service" (feature)
Copy: [1-2 sentences explaining the benefit and proof]
Example: "Most customers finish in under 30 minutes. You get your morning back."

Block 2: [Different benefit]
Block 3: [Different benefit]

---

SOCIAL PROOF SECTION
Headline: "Trusted by [Number] Happy Customers"

Proof Element (choose 1-2):
- Customer testimonial: "[Name], [Company]" + quote
- Star rating: "4.9★ on Google from 250+ reviews"
- Case study callout: "Increased sales by 35% in first 3 months"
- Client logos: "Used by [Brand], [Brand], [Brand]"
- Guarantee: "Clog-free for 12 months or we'll clean free"

---

FINAL CTA SECTION
Headline: [Action-focused, low barrier]
Example: "Ready to Get Started?"
Example: "Try Free for 30 Days"

Copy: [1-2 sentences, address final objections]
Example: "No credit card required. 100% money-back guarantee if not satisfied."

CTA: [Same as hero CTA or specific]
Example: "Book Your Free Inspection Now"
```

## FAQ Page Template

```
PAGE HEADLINE
"Frequently Asked Questions"

QUESTION CATEGORIES:
- General (What do you do, how do you work, etc.)
- Pricing & Guarantees
- [Service-specific] (e.g., "About Gutter Cleaning")
- Booking & Service
- Guarantees & Warranty

FORMAT (for each Q&A):
Q: [Common customer question or objection]
A: [Direct, helpful answer that addresses the concern and includes CTA if appropriate]

EXAMPLE Q&A SET (Gutter Cleaning):

Q: "Do I really need gutter cleaning?"
A: "Yes. Clogged gutters cause water damage to your foundation, siding, and fascia. Regular cleaning costs ~$150-300/year. Water damage costs $5,000+. It's the cheapest maintenance you'll do."

Q: "How often should I clean my gutters?"
A: "Most homes need cleaning 1-2 times/year. If you have lots of trees, 3-4 times/year. We recommend a free inspection to see what your gutters need."

Q: "Are you insured?"
A: "Yes, fully licensed and insured. You're protected if anything goes wrong."

Q: "Can you do it the same day?"
A: "Usually yes! We typically complete 95% of requests within 24 hours. Call for availability."

Q: "What if my gutters clog again after cleaning?"
A: "We guarantee zero-clog for 30 days. If they clog within 30 days of our cleaning, we re-clean free."

Q: "How much does it cost?"
A: "Prices vary based on home size and gutter condition. Most single-story homes are $150-250. Get a free estimate: [CTA]"

---

FINAL SECTION:
"Still have questions?"
CTA: "Contact us" or "Call [phone number]"
```

## Using These Templates

### Step 1: Choose the Right Template
- Homepage → Homepage Template
- Service Page → Services/Product Page Template
- Company Page → About Us Template
- Customer Reviews → Testimonials Template
- Answer Questions → FAQ Template

### Step 2: Customize for Your Positioning
Replace [brackets] with:
- Your positioning statement and messaging pillars
- Real customer testimonials and case study results
- Actual benefits from market research
- Real team members and credentials

### Step 3: Match Your Brand Voice
- Replace generic language with your brand voice
- Use customer language (how do THEY talk about the problem?)
- Add personality that matches your positioning

### Step 4: Review Against Quality Standards
- Is it benefit-focused (not feature-heavy)?
- Are CTAs clear and action-oriented?
- Is copy scannable (short paragraphs, strong subheadings)?
- Does it follow the messaging pillars from the brief?

---

## Template Best Practices

✅ **Do**:
- Keep paragraphs to 2-3 sentences max
- Use benefit-focused language
- Include specific numbers/proof when possible
- Make CTAs visible and action-oriented
- Use real customer testimonials (not generic)
- Include multiple ways to contact/convert

❌ **Don't**:
- Use buzzwords or corporate jargon
- Focus on features instead of benefits
- Make paragraphs longer than 4 sentences
- Hide CTAs or make them unclear
- Generic testimonials ("Great service!" - John)
- Missing contact info or CTAs

## When to Customize Further

These templates work as-is for most pages, but customize more if:
- Your positioning is highly specific (e.g., niche vertical)
- You have unique customer data (conversion patterns, pain points)
- Your brand voice has strong personality
- Your industry has unique requirements (heavily regulated, etc.)

The design team may also tweak copy layout and formatting. These templates are starting points, not final copy.
