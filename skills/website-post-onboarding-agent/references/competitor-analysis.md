# Competitor Analysis Methodology

## Overview

Competitor analysis is the foundation of positioning strategy. By understanding what competitors are claiming, how they're targeting, and what gaps they're leaving, we can find where the client can own a unique position.

**Goal**: Analyze 5-7 direct competitors to identify positioning gaps.

**Output**: Competitive matrix showing how competitors differ on key dimensions.

---

## Finding Competitors

### Who Is a Direct Competitor?

**Yes, direct competitor**:
- Same service/product category
- Same or overlapping target audience
- Similar price range
- Competing for same customers

**Example**: For Clean Cut Gutters, direct competitors are other gutter cleaning services in the same region/market.

**No, not a direct competitor**:
- Adjacent services (roofing, landscaping) unless they also offer gutters
- National aggregators (Angie's List) unless they're the customer's primary alternative
- Massive corporations (if the client is SMB)

### Finding 5-7 Competitors

Use these methods:
1. **Google Search**: Search primary keywords (e.g., "gutter cleaning [city]")
2. **Review Sites**: Check Google, Yelp, Angie's List for top-rated competitors
3. **Industry Directories**: Industry-specific platforms (e.g., HomeAdvisor for home services)
4. **LinkedIn**: Search competitors in the industry
5. **Direct Competitor Mentions**: Who does the client consider their competition?

### Prioritization

If more than 7 competitors found, focus on:
- Top 3-4 by market share / online presence
- 2-3 mid-tier competitors with different positioning
- 1-2 niche competitors with unique angles

## Competitor Analysis Framework

For each competitor, analyze these dimensions:

### 1. **Positioning Statement** (What they claim)
Read their homepage, tagline, and mission.

**Example**:
- Clean & Green Gutter Service: "Eco-friendly gutter cleaning with zero waste"
- Pro Gutter Pros: "Same-day gutter cleaning for busy professionals"
- Local Gutters Co: "Trusted family-owned gutter service since 1985"

**How to extract**:
- What's the headline promise?
- What problem do they say they solve?
- What's their primary differentiator?

### 2. **Target Audience** (Who they're going after)
Infer from website messaging, testimonials, imagery.

**Examples**:
- Eco-conscious homeowners (Green positioning targets environmentalists)
- Busy professionals / time-stressed homeowners (Same-day targets convenience seekers)
- Families wanting local trust (Family-owned targets people who value community relationships)

**How to infer**:
- Who do the testimonials feature?
- What pain points do they address?
- What imagery and language do they use?

### 3. **Key Differentiators** (Why they're different)
What do they emphasize as unique?

**Examples**:
- **Eco-friendly**: "100% compostable debris, zero plastic waste"
- **Speed**: "Same-day service guaranteed or your next visit free"
- **Trust/Reputation**: "35 years of 5-star reviews"
- **Warranty**: "Clog-free guarantee for 6 months"

**How to find**:
- What do they repeat across pages?
- What's in their "About" section?
- What's emphasized in customer testimonials?

### 4. **Pricing Model**
What pricing approach do they use?

**Examples**:
- Per-visit pricing ($150-300 per cleaning)
- Subscription model ($49/month for 4x annual)
- Tiered pricing (Basic, Premium, Elite)
- Free estimates with proposal-based pricing

**How to find**:
- Check their "Services" or "Pricing" page
- Read customer reviews (often mention price)
- Call and ask for pricing structure

### 5. **Homepage Structure** (How they tell their story)
What sections do they use and in what order?

**Example structure**:
1. Hero: Headline + CTA
2. Problem statement: "Clogged gutters cause..."
3. Solution introduction: "We specialize in..."
4. Key differentiators: 3-4 benefits with icons
5. Social proof: Testimonials + ratings
6. CTA: "Schedule free estimate"

**How to analyze**:
- Scroll through homepage
- Note section order and purpose
- Identify primary and secondary CTAs

### 6. **Primary CTA** (What action do they want?)
What's the main conversion goal?

**Examples**:
- "Schedule Free Estimate"
- "Call Now for Same-Day Service"
- "Get Your Cleaning Quote"
- "Join Our Subscription Program"

**How to identify**:
- What's the biggest/most visible button?
- What action do they emphasize?
- Where are CTAs placed?

## Positioning Gaps Analysis

### Step 1: Cluster Competitors
Group by positioning dimension:

**By Differentiation**:
- Speed/Convenience: Pro Gutter Pros, National Gutter Systems
- Eco/Sustainability: Clean & Green
- Trust/Local: Local Gutters Co
- Price: Budget Gutters

**By Target Audience**:
- Busy professionals: Pro Gutter Pros, National Gutter Systems
- Environmentally conscious: Clean & Green
- Families seeking trust: Local Gutters Co
- Cost-conscious: Budget Gutters

### Step 2: Identify Gaps
Where are clusters sparse? What pain points are underserved?

**Gap examples**:
- No one is claiming "hassle-free rescheduling" (pain point: scheduling is frustrating)
- No one is claiming "guaranteed zero-clog for 12 months" (pain point: recurring clogs are expensive)
- No one targets commercial/multi-family buildings (segment gap)
- No one combines "eco-friendly + affordable" (positioning gap)

### Step 3: Recommendation
Recommend positioning that:
- Fills an identified gap
- Targets an underserved audience
- Differentiates from 5-7 competitors
- Is defensible (client can deliver)

**Example recommendation**:
"Position as 'The gutter service for property managers,' because:
- Competitors target residential homeowners exclusively
- Commercial/multi-family is underserved
- Pain point is different (bulk properties need reliability + scalability)
- Pricing model can be subscription-based (recurring revenue)
- Client has capacity to serve 20+ properties (proof)"

## Data Sources for Competitor Analysis

- **Website**: Primary source (messaging, structure, CTAs)
- **Google Reviews / Yelp**: Customer perception, pain points mentioned
- **LinkedIn**: Company info, team, company size
- **Local directories**: Google Business, BBB, industry-specific platforms
- **Social Media**: Brand personality, how they engage customers
- **Reddit / Forums**: Customer discussions about the category
- **Press / Articles**: How competitors are positioned in media

---

## Common Pitfalls

❌ **Analyzing the wrong competitors**: Comparing to aggregators (Angie's List) or adjacent services instead of direct competitors

❌ **Surface-level analysis**: Just noting the logo and name, not actually reading messaging

❌ **Too few competitors**: Analyzing only 2-3 competitors doesn't reveal gaps

❌ **Confusing features with benefits**: "They use advanced tools" (feature) vs. "They guarantee zero-clog for 6 months" (benefit)

❌ **Ignoring target audience**: Competitors might position differently for different audiences

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] 5-7 direct competitors analyzed
- [ ] Positioning statement extracted for each
- [ ] Target audience inferred with supporting evidence
- [ ] Key differentiators identified (not just features)
- [ ] Pricing model documented
- [ ] Primary CTA noted
- [ ] Competitive matrix is clear and comparable
- [ ] Positioning gaps explicitly identified
- [ ] Gap analysis includes both "what" (what's missing) and "why" (why it's an opportunity)
- [ ] Recommendation tied to gap analysis
