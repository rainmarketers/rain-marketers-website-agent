# Example Website Brief: Clean Cut Gutters

This is a real example of the Website Brief output from the Website Post-Onboarding Agent. Use this as a reference when reviewing actual client briefs.

---

## Executive Summary

**Client**: Clean Cut Gutters  
**Market**: Residential gutter cleaning in Chicago metro  
**Target Audience**: Homeowners aged 40-65 with homes built before 2000  
**Recommended Positioning**: The gutter cleaning service that guarantees zero clogs for 12 months, because we use proprietary inspection technology and offer same-day service with a lifetime warranty.

**Why This Positioning**:
- Competitors emphasize "reliability" and "experience" (commodity positioning)
- Zero clog guarantee is unclaimed by top 5 competitors
- Same-day service differentiates from "call and wait 1-2 weeks"
- Lifetime warranty addresses customer's primary fear: "Will gutters clog again next year?"
- Data shows "no hassle" and "peace of mind" are top decision drivers for target audience

---

## Messaging Pillars

Based on the positioning and market research, these are the 3 core messages to reinforce:

**Pillar 1: Peace of Mind**
The customer's primary pain point is worrying about water damage. Position as the service that removes that worry with a guarantee.
- *Message*: "Stop worrying. Zero clogs guaranteed for 12 months or we clean free."
- *Where*: Hero section, testimonials, guarantee section

**Pillar 2: Same-Day Convenience**
Decision criteria analysis shows "availability" is #2 factor after trust. Emphasize speed.
- *Message*: "Most customers scheduled and completed in 24 hours. Call today, done tomorrow."
- *Where*: Service page, CTA copy, FAQ

**Pillar 3: Expert Inspection**
Differentiate from DIY and cheaper alternatives by emphasizing professional inspection.
- *Message*: "Our proprietary inspection camera catches issues before they cause damage. See your gutters in high-definition."
- *Where*: Services page, process section, differentiators

## Homepage Structure

Recommended sections and flow:

### 1. **Hero Section** (the promise)
- Headline: "Never Worry About Clogged Gutters Again"
- Subheadline: "Same-day service, zero-clog guarantee. Backed by 20+ years of expertise."
- Primary CTA: "Schedule Your Free Inspection"
- Hero image: Before/after of clean gutters, or technician doing inspection

### 2. **The Problem** (empathize)
- Headline: "Clogged gutters damage your home"
- Copy: "Water damage costs $5,000+ to repair. Most homeowners forget to clean until it's too late."
- Why it matters: Establishes urgency and proves the positioning ("peace of mind") is valuable

### 3. **Our Solution** (introduce positioning)
- Headline: "Gutters that stay clean. Guaranteed."
- Copy: "Same-day cleaning, zero-clog guarantee for 12 months. If they clog, we re-clean free."
- Why it matters: Directly addresses the pain point with differentiated promise

### 4. **Value Propositions** (support the positioning)

**Block 1: Same-Day Service**
- Icon: Clock
- Headline: "Done in 24 Hours, Usually Today"
- Copy: "Most requests completed same or next day. No waiting 2+ weeks."

**Block 2: Expert Inspection**
- Icon: Magnifying glass / Camera
- Headline: "See Your Gutters in HD"
- Copy: "Our inspection camera shows exactly what we're doing and catches problems before they cause damage."

**Block 3: Lifetime Guarantee**
- Icon: Shield / Checkmark
- Headline: "Zero-Clog Guarantee"
- Copy: "If your gutters clog within 12 months, we re-clean free. You're protected."

### 5. **Social Proof** (build trust)
- Headline: "Trusted by 2,000+ Chicago Homeowners"
- Proof: "4.8★ on Google from 250+ reviews"
- Sample testimonial: "John cleaned my gutters in 2 hours and explained everything. Worth every penny." – Mary, Chicago

### 6. **Before & After Gallery**
- 4-6 before/after photos of recent work
- Builds credibility and shows quality of work

### 7. **FAQ Section** (address objections)
- Q: "How much does it cost?"  A: "Most homes $150-300. Get a free estimate."
- Q: "Are you insured?"  A: "Fully licensed and insured for your protection."
- Q: "What if gutters clog again?"  A: "We re-clean free within 12 months. Zero-clog guarantee."

### 8. **Final CTA** (close)
- Headline: "Ready to Stop Worrying?"
- Copy: "Call today for same-day availability."
- CTA: "Schedule Your Free Inspection"

## Call-to-Action Strategy

**Primary CTA**: "Schedule Your Free Inspection"
- Why this CTA: "Free" removes objection. "Inspection" is less commitment than "hire us."
- Placement: Hero, navbar, footer, multiple CTAs throughout
- Destination: Online booking or phone number for same-day availability

**Secondary CTA**: "Get Your Free Quote"
- Why this CTA: For hesitant visitors who want pricing before calling
- Placement: Services page, FAQ section
- Destination: Quote request form or phone number

**Action verbs**: "Schedule," "Call Now," "Get Started," "Book Today"  
**Avoid**: "Contact," "Submit," "Learn More" (too vague)

---

## Success Metrics (KPIs)

Website should track and optimize for:

1. **Lead Volume**: 15+ quality leads per month (calls + quote requests)
   - Baseline: Currently 5-8 leads/month from Google Business
   - Target: 200% increase from new website

2. **Booking Conversion**: 20%+ of leads become booked appointments
   - Baseline: Not currently tracked; estimate 15%
   - Current bottleneck: Long response time (24-48 hours) loses leads

3. **Google Business Profile**: 50+ reviews/year with 4.7★+ rating
   - Baseline: Currently 250 reviews at 4.8★
   - Goal: Maintain rating, grow review volume

4. **Cost Per Lead**: Maintain <$10 CAC (customer acquisition cost)
   - Baseline: Google Business is essentially free organic
   - Goal: Website should drive additional organic leads at low cost

**Primary metric to optimize for**: Booking conversion rate (% of leads → scheduled appointments)  
**Secondary metric**: Lead volume (total calls + quote requests)

## Appendix: Market Research Supporting This Brief

### Market Position
- Gutter cleaning is a $7B+ market in North America
- Chicago metro has 1.2M single-family homes (potential market)
- Average homeowner spends $200-400/year on gutter maintenance
- Peak season: Spring/Fall (as shown by Google Trends data)

### Competitor Positioning
- **Competitor A (Local Gutter Pros)**: "35 years of family-owned trust"
- **Competitor B (Pro Gutter Systems)**: "Fast, professional service"
- **Competitor C (Affordable Gutters)**: "Lowest price in Chicago"
- **Competitor D (Handyman Connection)**: "One-stop home services"

**Gap**: None are claiming "zero-clog guarantee" or emphasizing "inspection technology."

### Customer Pain Points (from Google reviews, Reddit, surveys)
1. "Don't remember when to clean" → Our 12-month guarantee solves this
2. "Don't trust unknown contractors" → Our 20+ year track record + reviews solve this
3. "Getting someone to come is a hassle" → Our same-day service solves this
4. "Previous contractor did bad work" → Our guarantee + inspection solve this

### Target Audience Validation
- Chicago metro homeowners aged 40-65
- Median home value: $350K+ (can afford premium service)
- Prefer local, trusted vendors (as shown by Google Business review patterns)
- Value convenience (as shown by willingness to pay 20% premium for same-day)
