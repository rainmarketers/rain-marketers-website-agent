## Client Context (Onboarding Form)

### Company Basics
- **Name**: Clean Cut Gutters
- **Website**: https://cleancutgutters.com
- **Business Type**: B2C Services
- **Industry**: Gutter cleaning
- **Years in Business**: 8

### Target Customer
- **Primary**: Homeowners aged 40-65 in Chicago suburbs with homes built before 2000
- **Pain Points**:
  - Don't remember to clean gutters
  - Fear of climbing ladders / don't want to DIY
  - Don't trust unfamiliar contractors
  - Need reliable, local service

### Market & Competition
- **Service Area**: Chicago metro (30-mile radius)
- **Decision Criteria**: Local presence, reputation/reviews, speed of service, warranty/guarantee, transparent pricing
- **Competitive Advantage**: Same-day service, 20+ years founder experience, free gutter inspection with photos
- **Known Competitors**: Handyman Connection, Local Gutter Pros, Affordable Gutter Service

### Current Marketing & Goals
- **Current Channels**: Google Business (4.8★ rating), referrals, some Facebook
- **Website Goals**: Generate leads (phone calls and quote requests)
- **Success Metrics**: 15+ quality leads per month, 20% call-to-booking conversion
- **Budget/Timeline**: $20K budget, Q2 launch

### Additional Context
- **Founder Story**: Started as side business while working corporate job; grew to 4 employees through word-of-mouth and Google reviews
- **Current Tagline**: "Same-day gutter cleaning you can trust"

## What's in This Example

### 1. `brief.md`
The complete output from the Website Post-Onboarding Agent, including:

**Sections**:
- Executive summary
- Target customer profile
- Market research & competitive analysis
- Positioning strategy
- Website brief (messaging, CTAs, KPIs)
- Content templates (homepage, services, testimonials, FAQ)
- Sitemap recommendations

**Key findings**:
- **Positioning**: "Same-day gutter service for busy professionals"
- **Messaging Pillars**:
  1. Speed: Same-day or next-day service guaranteed
  2. Reliability: Licensed, insured, lifetime guarantee
  3. Trustworthiness: Local presence, personal relationships, 20+ years experience
- **Competitive Gap**: Competitors either emphasize speed OR local trust, not both
- **Recommended Sitemap**: 7 main pages (services, why choose us, process, testimonials, service area, FAQ, contact)

## Key Learnings

### What Makes This Brief Strong

✅ **Specific positioning** – Not just "reliable gutter cleaning" but "same-day for busy professionals"  
✅ **Defensible differentiation** – Based on competitive research, not assumptions  
✅ **Clear audience** – Specific age, location, psychographics, pain points  
✅ **Proof-focused** – Every claim backed by founder experience or founder story  
✅ **Actionable templates** – Ready-to-customize copy, not generic frameworks  
✅ **Measurement-oriented** – KPIs tied to business goals (leads per month, conversion rates)

### What to Adapt for Your Client

🔄 **Positioning**: Yours will be different based on competitive gaps  
🔄 **Messaging Pillars**: Adjust to match client's actual differentiators  
🔄 **Sitemap**: May need more/fewer pages based on business complexity  
🔄 **Content Templates**: Will be customized to their industry/positioning  
🔄 **KPIs**: Should align with client's success metrics, not copied
